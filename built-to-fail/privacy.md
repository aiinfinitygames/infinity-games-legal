# Privacy Policy — Built to Fail

**Effective date:** 2026-08-05 *(previous version: 2026-07-16)*
**Publisher:** Infinity Games
**Contact:** ai.infinitygames@gmail.com

This is the privacy policy for the mobile application **Built to Fail: Merge & Tinker** ("the App"), published by Infinity Games ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, and which third parties receive it.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Built to Fail has **no game server** — your progress lives on your device.

> **What changed on 2026-08-05:** our advertising provider changed from **Google AdMob**
> to **Unity Ads**. No new *category* of data is collected. Ads are still limited to
> rewarded and interstitial formats, and there is still exactly one advertising company
> in the chain.

---

## 1. Data we collect

### 1.1 Local game state (stored on your device only)
The App stores the following on your device only — it is **never** sent to us:

- Coin and Gem balances
- The Invention Catalog (which machines you have discovered)
- Owned and selected workshop themes (cosmetics)
- Workshop upgrades, generator level, and bench-expansion progress
- Current energy and the energy-refill timer
- Daily-reward streak and cycle progress
- Audio settings (Music, SFX)
- Whether ads have been removed (if you make the optional purchase)
- A launch counter used only to pace how often an ad is shown

You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the App.

### 1.2 Advertising data
The App is free and supported by ads served through **Unity Ads**. Ads appear as optional **rewarded** ads (for example, to top up energy or double a daily reward) and occasional **interstitial** ads between sessions. To show and measure ads, Unity may collect your Android Advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad-interaction data. See Section 3 for what control you have over this.

### 1.3 Analytics data
The App uses **GameAnalytics** to understand how the game is played (e.g. merges, new-machine discoveries, order completions, daily claims, running out of energy, ad and reward events, and catalog-progression milestones). This data is collected in anonymous/aggregated form together with basic device information (model, OS version, country/region). It is not linked to your identity and is used only to improve the game.

### 1.4 In-app purchases
If you make an optional purchase (e.g. a Gem pack, or remove ads), the transaction is handled by **Google Play Billing**. We receive confirmation of the purchase from Google; we do not receive or store your payment-card details.

## 3. Personalized ads & consent

Ads in this App are served by **Unity Ads**, which is a single advertising network rather
than a mediation layer.

**The App does not currently show an in-app consent prompt, and Settings contains no
personalized-ads toggle.** Earlier versions did, through Google's consent tool; that tool
was part of the Google AdMob integration and stopped being available to us when that
integration was retired. Unity Ads provides no replacement consent interface, so the
previous control no longer exists rather than merely having moved.

What you can still do, at the device level, applies to every app on your phone:

- **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID** removes the identifier
  entirely; apps then cannot use it to build a profile across sessions.
- **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization** signals to
  advertising networks that they should not personalize.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and want your data handled under a specific consent
choice, please contact us at ai.infinitygames@gmail.com and we will action it for your
device.

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **Local game state**: kept on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We never receive it.
- **Advertising and analytics data**: retained by Unity Ads and GameAnalytics per their own policies (linked above). We only ever access it in aggregated form.

The App itself operates no backend server and stores no per-user records on our side.

## 6. Your rights

You can:
- **Stop all data collection**: uninstall the App.
- **Limit ad personalization**: use the Android system controls described in Section 3 — the App itself no longer offers this setting.
- **Reset your Advertising ID**: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID. This severs the link Unity Ads can build between sessions.
- **Request data deletion / make a privacy request**: email us at ai.infinitygames@gmail.com. For data held by Unity Ads or GameAnalytics, you may also use the contacts in their policies.

Depending on your region (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing, and to opt out of certain processing.
