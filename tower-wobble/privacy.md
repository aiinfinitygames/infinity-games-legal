# Privacy Policy — Tower Wobble

**Effective date:** 2026-06-04
**Publisher:** Infinity Games
**Contact:** ai.infinitygames@gmail.com

This is the privacy policy for the mobile application **Tower Wobble** ("the App"), published by Infinity Games ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, and which third parties receive it.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Tower Wobble has **no game server** — your progress lives on your device.

---

## 1. Data we collect

### 1.1 Local game state (stored on your device only)
The App stores the following on your device only — it is **never** sent to us:

- Best score, highest biome reached, and coin balance
- Owned and selected cosmetic skins
- Audio and gameplay settings (Music, SFX, Ghost Race, Reduce Effects, personalized-ads consent)
- Daily-reward streak, daily-mission progress, and unlocked achievements
- Your saved "ghost" run (a local recording of your best run that you race against — it never leaves your device)
- Whether ads have been removed (if you make the optional purchase)

You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the App.

### 1.2 Advertising data
The App is free and supported by ads served through **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your Android Advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad-interaction data. With your consent, ads may be **personalized**; if you decline, ads are served in **non-personalized (contextual)** mode. See Section 3 for how to change this.

### 1.3 Analytics data
The App uses **GameAnalytics** to understand how the game is played (e.g. run start/end, score, biome reached, ad and reward events, longest streak). This data is collected in anonymous/aggregated form together with basic device information (model, OS version, country/region). It is not linked to your identity and is used only to improve the game.

### 1.4 In-app purchases
If you make an optional purchase (e.g. remove ads), the transaction is handled by **Google Play Billing**. We receive confirmation of the purchase from Google; we do not receive or store your payment-card details.

---

## 3. Personalized ads & consent

On first launch (and changeable at any time), the App asks whether you consent to **personalized ads**:

- **Consent given** — ads may be personalized using the data in Section 1.2.
- **Consent declined** — ads are served in non-personalized (contextual) mode only.

You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Personalized Ads**. You can also reset or opt out of your Advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

---

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **Local game state**: kept on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We never receive it.
- **Advertising and analytics data**: retained by AdMob and GameAnalytics per their own policies (linked above). We only ever access it in aggregated form.

The App itself operates no backend server and stores no per-user records on our side.

---

## 5. Children

This App is rated for ages 13+ in the Google Play Store, consistent with AdMob's terms of service. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used this App, please contact us at ai.infinitygames@gmail.com and we will address it.

## 6. Your rights

You can:
- **Stop all data collection**: uninstall the App.
- **Change personalized-ads consent**: Settings → Personalized Ads.
- **Reset your Advertising ID**: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID. This severs the link AdMob can build between sessions.
- **Request data deletion / make a privacy request**: email us at ai.infinitygames@gmail.com. For data held by AdMob or GameAnalytics, you may also use the contacts in their policies.

Depending on your region (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing, and to opt out of certain processing.
