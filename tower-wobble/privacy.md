## 1. Data we collect

### 1.1 Local game state (stored on your device only)
The App stores the following on your device only — it is **never** sent to us:

- Best score, highest biome reached, and coin balance
- Owned and selected cosmetic skins
- Audio and gameplay settings (Music, SFX, Ghost Race, Reduce Effects)
- Daily-reward streak, daily-mission progress, and unlocked achievements
- Your saved "ghost" run (a local recording of your best run that you race against — it never leaves your device)
- Whether ads have been removed (if you make the optional purchase)

You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the App.

### 1.2 Advertising data
The App is free and supported by ads served through **Unity Ads**. To show and measure ads, Unity may collect your Android Advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad-interaction data. See Section 3 for what control you have over this.

### 1.3 Analytics data
The App uses **GameAnalytics** to understand how the game is played (e.g. run start/end, score, biome reached, ad and reward events, longest streak). This data is collected in anonymous/aggregated form together with basic device information (model, OS version, country/region). It is not linked to your identity and is used only to improve the game.

### 1.4 In-app purchases
If you make an optional purchase (e.g. remove ads), the transaction is handled by **Google Play Billing**. We receive confirmation of the purchase from Google; we do not receive or store your payment-card details.

## 3. Personalized ads & consent

Ads in this App are served by **Unity Ads**, which is a single advertising network rather
than a mediation layer.

**Consent is collected by Unity Ads itself.** Where the law requires it — including the
EEA and the UK — the Unity Ads SDK presents its own opt-in for behaviourally targeted
advertising the first time an ad is shown. We do not run a separate prompt of our own,
and Settings no longer carries a personalized-ads toggle: earlier versions had one that
opened Google's consent tool, which was part of the Google AdMob integration and went
away with it. Removing that button changed which component asks you, not whether you
are asked.

If you decline, ads are served without behavioural targeting rather than not at all.

You can revisit the choice at any time:

- **During any ad**, select the **Unity Data Privacy icon**. From there you can opt out,
  request access to your data, or ask for it to be deleted.
- **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID** removes the identifier
  entirely; apps then cannot use it to build a profile across sessions.
- **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization** signals to every
  advertising network on the device that it should not personalize.

You can also contact us at ai.infinitygames@gmail.com and we will action a request for
your device.

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **Local game state**: kept on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We never receive it.
- **Advertising and analytics data**: retained by Unity Ads and GameAnalytics per their own policies (linked above). We only ever access it in aggregated form.

The App itself operates no backend server and stores no per-user records on our side.

## 6. Your rights

You can:
- **Stop all data collection**: uninstall the App.
- **Limit ad personalization**: use the Unity Data Privacy icon shown during any ad, or the Android system controls — both are described in Section 3.
- **Reset your Advertising ID**: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID. This severs the link Unity Ads can build between sessions.
- **Request data deletion / make a privacy request**: email us at ai.infinitygames@gmail.com. For data held by Unity Ads or GameAnalytics, you may also use the contacts in their policies.

Depending on your region (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing, and to opt out of certain processing.
